Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose FitFlame and tap Cancel Subscription. If you're on the free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid being charged. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through the App Store, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed, but FitFlame still shows the paywall.

Open FitFlame's Settings and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you subscribed with. If it still doesn't unlock, send us a message with the topic "Subscription or billing".

How do I keep my fits when I switch phones?

Tap Sign in with Apple in FitFlame's Settings. Your fits are backed up and come back when you sign in on your new phone. Without signing in, fits are saved only on this phone and are lost if you delete the app.

Why does FitFlame want my location?

Your judge checks the weather where you are, so the roast knows what your outfit is up against. FitFlame uses your approximate location only, and only for weather. Prefer not to share it? Pick a city in FitFlame's Settings instead.

My judge said something that wasn't okay.

Judges are supposed to roast the clothes, never the person. If a roast crossed the line, send us a message with what was said and which judge said it. It helps us make FitFlame better.

It says I've reached today's roast limit.

To keep FitFlame running smoothly for everyone, there's a generous daily limit on roasts. It resets the next day.

How do I delete my account and data?

In FitFlame, open Settings and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and any fits backed up to our servers. To remove fits saved on your phone, use Delete all fits or delete the app. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription; cancel it in your iPhone's Settings.

How do I ask about my personal data?

Choose "Privacy request" in the form. If you signed in with Apple, send it from the email you used so we can find your account. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
