Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

FitFlame ("FitFlame", "we", "us") is an iPhone app that roasts your outfit with AI judges. This policy explains what information the app and this website collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the FitFlame iOS app and to getfitflame.com.

The short version: we use your outfit photo and local weather to make your roast, and the roast text to make your judge's voice. Your fits are saved on your phone, and only go to our servers if you sign in with Apple to back them up. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

1. Information we collect

Photos you choose to roast

When you tap Roast My Fit, the photo you took or picked is sent to our servers and then to our AI provider to generate your roast. FitFlame only accesses the photos you choose; it does not scan your photo library.

Location and weather

If you allow location access, FitFlame uses your approximate location (never your precise location) on your phone to look up the weather with Apple's WeatherKit and to show your city's name. When you roast a fit, we send the city name and weather conditions (such as temperature, rain chance and wind) with your photo so your judge can comment on them. We do not send or store your coordinates on our servers. If you don't allow location access, you can pick a city instead.

Your account

  • Guest account. When you first open the app, FitFlame creates an anonymous account with a random ID. It isn't linked to your name or email, and it lets our servers know requests come from the app.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you sign in, we receive a user identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email address. Apple lets you hide your real email by sharing a private relay address instead.

Saved fits

Your photo, roast, voice recording and the weather at the time are saved on your phone in Previous Fits (you can turn this off in Settings). If you sign in with Apple, these are also backed up to our servers so they follow you to other devices.

Subscription information

Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your account has an active subscription or free trial. RevenueCat receives your FitFlame account ID and your App Store purchase history for FitFlame.

Usage and technical information

Our servers count how many roasts and voices each account makes per day so we can apply fair-use limits, and keep short-term service logs (such as request times and errors) to run and secure the service. FitFlame does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Support messages

If you contact us through the support form on this website, we receive the email address, name (if given) and message you send, and use them only to reply to you.

2. How we use information

  • To generate your roast, outfit recommendation and spoken voice.
  • To show weather for your area.
  • To save, back up and restore your fits.
  • To provide your subscription and free trial, and to check that roasts come from subscribed accounts.
  • To prevent abuse, apply daily limits, fix problems and keep the service secure.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not use your photos to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

3. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that run FitFlame, and only what each one needs. The app asks for your permission before your first photo is sent to our AI providers.

Provider What they receive Why
Anthropic (Claude) Your outfit photo, city name and weather conditions Writes your roast and recommendation
ElevenLabs The text of your roast Turns the roast into your judge's voice
Supabase Your account, daily usage counts, service logs, and backed-up fits if you sign in with Apple Hosts our servers, accounts and storage
RevenueCat Your FitFlame account ID and App Store purchase history for FitFlame Manages subscriptions and free trials
Apple Approximate location (WeatherKit), sign-in and purchases Weather, Sign in with Apple, and App Store payments
Formspree Messages sent through our support form Delivers support messages to us

These providers process information on our behalf and under their own privacy and security commitments. AI providers may keep requests for a limited time, for example for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in their policies. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a merger or sale of FitFlame, in which case this policy would continue to apply.

4. How long we keep information

  • Fits on your phone stay until you delete them or delete the app.
  • Backed-up fits stay on our servers until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Photos sent only to make a roast (when you're not backing up) aren't stored on our servers after the roast is made.
  • Usage counts and service logs are kept only as long as needed to run and secure the service.
  • Your account is kept until you delete it.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Location: turn it off any time in the iPhone Settings app, or choose a city in FitFlame's Settings.
  • Saved fits: delete one fit, or all of them, in the app. You can also stop saving new fits in Settings.
  • Delete your account: in FitFlame, open Settings and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and any fits backed up to our servers. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel it in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Access, correction or other requests: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain uses. Send a request through our support page and choose "Privacy request". We'll respond within the time the law requires.

6. Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Backed-up fits are stored in private storage that only your account can read, and our AI and voice provider keys stay on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

7. Children

FitFlame isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used FitFlame, contact us through the support page and we'll delete their information.

8. International users

FitFlame's servers and service providers may be located in the United States and other countries. By using FitFlame, your information may be processed in countries other than where you live, with appropriate safeguards where required.

9. This website

getfitflame.com is hosted on GitHub Pages and doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app before they take effect.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests? Reach us through the support page.
